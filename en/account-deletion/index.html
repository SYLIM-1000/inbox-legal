Inbox Account and Guest Data Deletion

Effective date: 2026-09-25
Last revised: 2026-09-25

This page explains how to request deletion of a linked account or guest data for the Inbox mobile application (developer: Seungyong).

How to delete your account

Method 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Inbox app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. At the bottom, select Delete Account.
  4. Read the instructions and enter “DELETE”.
  5. Select Request Account Deletion.

When the request is received, you are immediately logged out and access to the account is blocked. Physical deletion of the account, content, and attachments starts immediately and is completed within 24 hours. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Method 2 — Request by email

If you cannot use the app, you may request deletion through the contact below. If you linked an account, include the linked email address. If you used Inbox only as a guest and lost the session, the Company may be unable to verify ownership of the notes or identify the deletion target from an email address or Apple app identity alone.

  • Email: pri1038@naver.com

Data that is deleted

Deleting guest or linked-account data deletes the following data owned by that User UUID.

  • Authentication information
  • All saved notes (title, summary, and original text)
  • Attached images and files
  • Categories, tags, and app settings

Data retained and retention periods

  • Access is blocked immediately upon a deletion request, and physical deletion of the account and content is completed within 24 hours.
  • We do not separately retain deleted content for 30 days for recovery purposes.
  • Completed deletion-job status and the recovery receipt hash are retained for up to 30 days after completion for deletion confirmation and limited incident response. They do not include content.
  • Minimal pseudonymous Apple transaction records may be retained for up to five years per event to comply with legally required transaction-record retention. They do not include your email, Inbox user ID, notes, or attachments and are not used to restore accounts or for marketing.
  • To prevent repeat grants of the free 10, the pseudonymous Apple app-identity digest and successful-free-analysis count are kept separately while the free-trial program operates to perform the Service agreement and prevent repeat free grants, then deleted when the program ends. They exclude email, the raw Apple identity, and notes/attachments and are not used for Content recovery, advertising, marketing, or tracking.
  • Account-link/transfer recovery and security-verification records are used until cleanup of copies resulting from an account transfer or cancellation is complete. Records for a completed or canceled transfer job are deleted within 30 days after copy cleanup has been verified complete. This does not mean original Content is separately retained for 30 days. Minimum records needed to link paid access follow the existing retention standards for paid access and restoration.

Apple subscriptions must be canceled separately

Deleting your Inbox account does not cancel an auto-renewing subscription in the Apple App Store.

  • Cancel it separately in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions or Apple Subscriptions.
  • For refunds, use the Apple refund request process.
  • You may request account deletion without canceling the subscription first.
  • Restore Purchases rechecks only currently verifiable monthly or lifetime access. It does not restore deleted data, inaccessible notes/attachments, or a lost guest session.
