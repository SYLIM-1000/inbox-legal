2. Purposes of Collection and Use

The Company uses collected personal information only for:

  1. Guest space and optional account linking — Create and maintain a private space and session without registration and, when chosen, link or securely transfer existing guest data and access to an account
  2. Service provision — Saving, viewing, searching, editing, and deleting User notes
  3. AI organization — Titles, summaries, tags, categories, and permitted image analysis through Apple PCC; and analysis of public URL text read by Jina and public YouTube captions read by the iOS app
  4. Apple access and free-use verification — Apply one shared 10-success allowance per verified Apple app identity; verify, restore, and synchronize monthly/lifetime and existing legacy access; and prevent repeat free grants
  5. Service operation and support — Error/crash diagnosis, sign-up/account-recovery guidance emails, User inquiries, and note/account deletion requests

The Company does not use personal information for purposes other than those above and obtains consent in advance if a purpose changes.

4. Provision to Third Parties

The Company does not provide Users’ personal information to third parties.

Exceptions are:

7. Destruction Procedure and Method

Procedure

When a User runs the in-Service account-deletion function, the User is immediately logged out and account access is blocked. Physical deletion of the Account, Content, and attachments begins immediately and is completed within 24 hours. Personal information whose retention period has expired or whose processing purpose has been fulfilled is destroyed without delay.

Method

  • Server data: On an account-deletion request, login sessions and access are blocked immediately; notes, categories, tags, and attachments are deleted from the database and storage within 24 hours. This is irreversible.
  • Individual Content: When note deletion is received, access to the note and attachments is blocked immediately and physical deletion occurs within 24 hours.
  • Limited retention: Minimal Apple transaction records and completed deletion jobs/recovery receipt hashes are retained only for the periods and purposes in Section 3.
  • Local cache/offline queue: The device cache and writing queue are deleted on sign-out, account switch, and account deletion, and are also removed when the app is deleted.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that prevents recovery or reproduction.

Guest-data deletion removes notes, categories, tags, attachments, and authentication information owned by the guest UUID. The pseudonymous Apple app-identity digest and successful-free-analysis count remain as a separate record while the free-trial program operates to perform the Service agreement and prevent repeat free grants, and are deleted when the program ends. They do not grant access to another User’s Content or recover deleted Content. Restore Purchases restores only verifiable access, not notes, attachments, or a lost guest session.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple auto-renewing subscription. Users may cancel separately in iOS Apple subscription management and may request account deletion regardless of cancellation.

8. Data-Subject (User) Rights and How to Exercise Them

Under the Personal Information Protection Act, Users may exercise these rights at any time.

  1. Request access — Saved notes and Account information can be viewed directly in the app.
  2. Request correction/deletion — Notes can be edited/deleted in the app, and the entire Account can be deleted through account deletion.
  3. Request suspension of processing — Users may request suspension of processing.

These rights may be exercised through in-app functions (account deletion and note editing/deletion) or by contacting Section 10. The Company will act within the period required by law. Users must be at least 14 years old; rights may also be exercised through a legal representative or similar person.

9. Measures to Ensure Security

The Company takes the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

  • Access control (row-level security, RLS): Database-level controls allow each User to access and process only their own data.
  • Encryption in transit: Communication between device and server is encrypted with TLS.
  • Stored-data protection: The device cache and offline queue are encrypted using iOS file protection (excluded from iCloud Backup), and passwords are hashed by the authentication processor.
  • Storage access restriction: Attachment storage is private and limited to the owner (User).

An App Attest key is scoped to an Inbox User UUID and app installation. Its public verification material and counter are used only to prevent forged or replayed proof. Apple app-verification information is not used for advertising, marketing, tracking, or profiling.

The Company does not currently represent that it has obtained a separate information-security certification (such as ISMS). This section will state one only if obtained.
